# TrueshotLodge

**The hunter's field guide to every tameable beast in WoW Forever.**

## Introduction

Every great pet starts with knowing where to look. TrueshotLodge puts the whole of hunter pet taming in one window,
right in the game: every pet ability and every rank, the beasts that teach them, where to find those beasts, which pet
trainers sell what, and a look inside your own stable, even when you are nowhere near a stable master.

Want Bite Rank 3 for your new cat? Open the ability, pick the rank, and TrueshotLodge lists the beasts that have it
when tamed, with their levels, their zones and a button that pins every spot they have been seen on your world map.
Wondering what your wolf in the stable could still learn? Your stable is kept in the addon, stats and abilities
included, so you can check it from anywhere.

Built for WoW Forever:

- **765+ tameable creatures** across **18 pet families**, with their levels, zones and map locations
- **Every look in 3D**: the 155 skins pets come in, each turnable in 3D, and which beasts wear it
- **Every pet ability and rank**: what it does, the pet level it needs, its training point cost and who can learn it
- **New in Forever**: creatures and families added in WoW Forever carry the ∞ mark
- **Verified tames**: a green dot marks creatures TrueshotLodge users have actually tamed, with the date on hover; a
  grey eye marks the ones only seen in the wild so far
- **Rare and elite** creatures stand out: rares glow gold, elites carry a skull
- **Pet tooltips**: hover a tameable beast in the world to see its family, attack speed, whether a hunter has tamed
  it (the green dot) and the abilities it teaches; each line can be turned off on its own
- **Pet ability tooltips**: a pet ability's tooltip (spellbook, pet bar, Beast Training, chat links) adds the pet
  level it needs and its training point cost
- Colors that mean the same thing everywhere: **yellow** is known, **green** is ready to learn, **grey** is not yet
  within reach

Open it from the minimap button or with **/tsl** (or **/trueshotlodge**).

Browse the same pets, abilities, looks and trainers on the web at **[TrueshotLodge.com](https://trueshotlodge.com)**.

## The UI

TrueshotLodge looks and feels like the game's own profession window: a list on the left, the details on the right,
and tabs down the side. The **search box** above the list looks for creatures: type part of a creature's name and the
list keeps only the ranks, families, looks and bounties that have a creature matching it, with just those creatures
on the right. Hold a side tab for a moment to drag it up or down the column and put your tabs in the order
you like (saved for each character). At the top right, across from the tab's title, are the **filter buttons**.
Each tab shows the filters it can use: **Level** (what your level reaches, or the
creatures you can tame now), **Zone** (only the zone you are standing in), **Pet** (only what your active pet's
family can learn), **Rares** (only the rare creatures), **Tamed** (only the creatures you have already tamed) and, on
the Trainers tab, **Faction**. A filter is grey while off, and red and glowing while on.
Beside the close button, the **gear** opens Options and the **?** opens Help (click either again to go back to your
tab).

## ![Families](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/ability_hunter_pet_wolf.jpg) Families

Every pet family, by type (Cunning, Ferocity, Tenacity), with its number of tameable creatures. Select one to see its
unique ability and what it does, its type and diet, the common and passive abilities it learns, and **all its tameable
creatures**, each with a **Map Location** button. The **Zone** and **Level** filters narrow the creatures to the
zone you are in and the ones you can tame now; a family with none left is not listed.

## ![Unique Abilities](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/ability_hunter_beasttaming.jpg) Unique Abilities

The abilities only one pet family can learn: Charge for boars, Prowl for cats, Lightning Breath for wind serpents, Web
for spiders and the rest. Each ability is headed by its family's icon, with its ranks underneath.

Select a rank to see:

- what it does, the pet level it needs, its training point cost and which family learns it
- **the tameable creatures that have that rank**, lowest level first, each with its level, its zone and a **Map
  Location** button that opens your world map with a pin on every place it has been seen
- whether you already know it; with **Known Ability Edit** on (Options, Advanced: for that session only), click the
  ability's icon to mark the rank known by hand (a rank the game has shown you know stays known). Known ranks are
  read from your own spells at login, with nothing to open

Turn on **Level** to show only the ranks your level has reached, **Zone** for only the ranks a creature in the zone
you are standing in has (each rank then counts its creatures there), or **Pet** for only the abilities your active
pet's family can learn.

## ![Common Abilities](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/ability_racial_cannibalize.jpg) Common Abilities

The abilities many families share, such as Bite, Claw, Cower, Dash, Dive and Growl, laid out the same way: every
rank, what it does, who can learn it, and the creatures you can tame it from. The **Level** and **Pet** filters work here too.

## ![Passive Abilities](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/spell_nature_unyeildingstamina.jpg) Passive Abilities

The passives your pet learns from a trainer: the five resistances, Great Stamina and Natural Armor, with every rank,
its level, its training point cost and the price a trainer charges for it.

## ![Passive Attack Speed](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_misc_pocketwatch_01.jpg) Passive Attack Speed

Every beast attacks at its own speed, and keeps it when tamed. This tab lists each attack speed (Faster Attack I to
VII, Slower Attack II and III) with all the creatures that have it, so you can hunt down the fastest pet of the family
you want. Every speed starts open, and the **Level** filter keeps the beasts you can tame now.

## ![Trainers](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/ability_hunter_beasttraining.jpg) Trainers

The pet trainers, grouped by zone and colored by faction. Select one to see their
title, level, location and coordinates, with a small map of the zone and a pin on the trainer; click it to open your
world map there. Turn on **Faction** to see only the trainers who will teach you.

## ![Looks](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/trade_archaeology_highbornesoulmirror.jpg) Looks

Every look each pet family comes in: the beasts that look alike (the same model and skin, whatever their size), the
same as [TrueshotLodge.com/looks](https://trueshotlodge.com/looks). Each family is a heading with its looks under it
and how many creatures have each; a look in **gold** has only one creature, the only place to get it. Select one to
see it in **3D**: drag to turn it, right-drag to move it and scroll to zoom. Under the model are the
creatures with that look, each with a **Map Location** button. The **Zone** and **Level** filters narrow them to the
zone you are in and the ones you can tame now, and the search finds the looks a creature has.

## ![Stable](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/ability_hunter_beastcall.jpg) Stable

Your pets, cached and kept in the addon so you can look them over anywhere, laid out like the game's own pet window:

- your **Current Pet** and your **Stabled Pets** slots across the top, with slots you have not bought yet shown in red
- the selected pet's name, level and family, with its stats: health, armor, damage, attack power, attack speed,
  movement speed, resistances and abilities, each with its icon (hover one for the full tooltip)
- its **3D model**, and its **experience bar**
- when you last visited a stable master

A pet is remembered the last time it was out, so even a pet sitting in the stable shows its stats and abilities.
When TrueshotLodge knows the beast a pet was tamed from, **Tamed From** names it, and clicking it opens its profile.

## ![Pet Profile](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/ability_hunter_beasttaming.jpg) Pet Profile

Click a creature's name anywhere in the window to open its profile: the creature lists under an ability, a family, a
bounty or a look, the Collected Data tab, and Tamed From on the Stable tab. The profile shows:

- its **Map** button, and its **3D model** (drag to turn it, right-drag to move it, scroll to zoom)
- its family, type, diet, level, attack speed and zone, whether it is new in Forever, who tamed it (hover for the
  date) and its NPC ID
- the **abilities** it teaches and its **looks**: click one to see it on its own tab (the family too)

**Target** targets the creature in game when it is near enough to see (not in combat, which the game does not allow),
and **Back** returns to the tab, selection or profile you came from.

## ![Bounties](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_inscription_80_contract_embers.jpg) Bounties

The beasts and abilities hunters most need found, posted as bounties: the same list as
[TrueshotLodge.com/bounties](https://trueshotlodge.com/bounties), worked out from the addon's data. How to claim one
is in Help (the **?** by the close button).

- **Pet Bounties**:
  - **Missing Rares**: rares no TrueshotLodge hunter has tamed yet
  - **Missing Normals**: every other creature no TrueshotLodge hunter has tamed yet
  - **Missing Forever**: the creatures new in WoW Forever (∞) no TrueshotLodge hunter has tamed yet
  - **Missing Attack Speed**: creatures whose attack speed nobody has recorded
  - **Missing Zone**: creatures nobody knows the whereabouts of
  - **Missing Model**: creatures whose look nobody knows yet, so the Looks tab cannot show them
- **Unique Ability Targets**: every rank of a family's unique ability, with how many known creatures have it and its
  **Potential Tameable Targets**: the family's untamed beasts between the levels of the ranks around it, which might
  have it
- **The Untamables**: the creatures hunters have uploaded that we keep out of the guide. Nothing to claim: they are
  listed so you can see everything uploads have brought in, including what we left out

Each bounty lists its creatures, the lowest level first, each with its level, family, zone and a **Map Location** button. Turn
on **Zone** to see only the ones in the zone you are in, or **Level** to see only the ones you can tame now, the
highest level first. The bounties under a heading are listed
alphabetically; right-click the heading to list them by number of creatures instead (and again to switch back).

To claim one: turn on Collect Mode, track the beast down (a look at its nameplate records its attack speed and where it
lives; a tame records the abilities it came with), then upload your `TrueshotLodge.lua` at
[TrueshotLodge.com](https://trueshotlodge.com/collector). We check what you found and add it for every hunter. With
**Send Name** on (Options, the gear by the close button: Collection), your tame is credited to you on the creature's Tamed badge: "Verified Tame:
date by *your name*".

## ![Database Search](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_misc_spyglass_03.jpg) Database Search

Every tameable creature, filtered and sorted however you like, as on
[TrueshotLodge.com/search](https://trueshotlodge.com/search), laid out like Options: the filters on the left, by
heading, and the matching creatures on the right, each with a **Map Location** button. Click one's name to open its
profile. Above the search box, **Level** is two small boxes (0 and 60 by default: every level); narrow it to a
range and the creatures with no level on file drop out, as a range on the website does. The search box itself
narrows the results by name, same as everywhere else, and the **Zone** and **Level** filter buttons work here too.

Each other filter is a row under its heading: click it to turn it on or off (its badge shows On or Off, or what is
chosen):

- **Taming**: Any, Tamed, Sighted (seen in the wild, not tamed) or Untamed
- **Forever**: Added in Forever only (∞)
- **Ability**: every ability on file, each its own row
- **Classification**: Normal, Rare, Elite, Rare Elite, Boss
- **Type** and **Diet**, read from the families on file
- **Family**: every pet family, each its own row
- **Sort**: Level, Name, Attack Speed, Classification or Tamed; click the one already in use (or right-click any of
  them) to reverse it

**Clear Filters**, at the top, resets everything on this tab, the search box and the Level boxes included.

### Database Search (Top)

An experimental second layout for the same tab, side by side with it for comparison: the same filters, but across
the top as dropdowns (closer to the website's own layout) instead of a list on the left, with the matching creatures
filling the full width underneath as a table (Creature, Family, Level, Attack Speed, Zone, Status), its column
headers clickable to sort by them (click again to reverse). Family, Type, Classification and Ability can each have
more than one thing checked at once; Diet and Taming pick one. Whichever layout is kept, the other (and its side
tab) is removed.

## ![Help](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_misc_questionmark.jpg) Help

Opened with the **?** by the close button:

- **How to Claim**: whether Collect Mode and Send Name are on, how many tames you have collected, and how a bounty is
  claimed
- **Filters**: what Level, Zone, Pet, Rares, Tamed and Faction do
- **Known Abilities**: how a rank becomes known (your own spells at login, learning it, Beast Training, or marking it by hand), with your counts
- **Commands**: the slash commands
- **Tab Descriptions**: what each tab is for

## ![Options](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_misc_gear_01.jpg) Options

Opened with the **gear** by the close button. The settings, grouped and explained, in the same list-and-details
layout:

- **General**: the minimap icon, and remembering your selections between tabs and sessions
- **Tooltips**: Pet Tooltips (tameable creatures and pets) and Pet Ability Tooltips (pet level and training points),
  each on or off, with a checkbox for each of its lines (family, attack speed, tamed, abilities, ...)
- **Visual**: the window's UI scale (50% to 150%) and resetting the tab order
- **Collection**: Collect Mode (see below), and Send Name: put your character's name on the beasts you tame, to be
  credited on the website (off by default: your finds stay anonymous)
- **Data Sharing**: who your collected finds are shared with (your shard, your zone, your group, the TrueshotLodge
  moderators), and whether to take in what other users share
- **Advanced**: debug messages, for troubleshooting, and Known Ability Edit: click an ability's icon to mark a rank
  known by hand (on for that session only)

## ![Collected Data](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_misc_note_02.jpg) Collected Data

Only shown while **Collect Mode** is on. TrueshotLodge's data grows with its players: with Collect Mode on, the addon
quietly records the beasts you tame (and the abilities they came with), the tameable creatures you meet in the wild
that the addon's data does not fully know yet (a new creature, level, zone or attack speed), and the pet trainers you
talk to. This tab lists all of it, with what is not yet in the addon's data highlighted. What you collect helps fill in
the gaps for every hunter, from missing attack speeds to creatures nobody has mapped yet.

## Help fill in the gaps

Some data is still missing: creatures whose attack speed, level or zone nobody has recorded, ranks of the families'
unique abilities with no known tameable creature, and rares no TrueshotLodge hunter has tamed yet. The
[Bounties](https://trueshotlodge.com/bounties) on the website, and the addon's Bounties tab, list them.

Turn on Collect Mode, go hunting, and upload your `TrueshotLodge.lua` at
[TrueshotLodge.com](https://trueshotlodge.com/collector). With Send Name on, the beasts you tame carry your name onto
the website's Tamed badge.
